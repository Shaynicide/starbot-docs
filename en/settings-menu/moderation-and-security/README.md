# 🛡️ Moderation & Security

#### [⚔️ Anti-Spam](anti-spam.md)

Stop repeated messages, mention spam, and other annoying behavior before it takes over chat.

Best for servers that want automatic protection without staff watching every message.

***

#### [🧾 Ban Appeals](ban-appeals.md)

Let banned users submit an appeal through Starbot.

Appeals can be reviewed by staff, making ban reviews easier to manage without messy DMs.

***

#### [🔔 Ping Control](ping-control.md)

Limit unwanted pings and control how Starbot handles role or user mentions.

Useful for reducing random ghost pings and keeping notifications under control.

***

#### [⚠️ Warnings](warnings.md)

Warn users and keep a record of moderation history.

Good for tracking repeat issues and keeping staff responses consistent.

***

#### [📜 Moderation Logs](moderation-logs.md)

Send moderation actions to a selected log channel.

Use this to keep a clear record of warnings, bans, kicks, deleted messages, and other staff actions
