# 🧑 Users

## Warn/Unwarn

Opens up the [warning](../settings-menu/moderation-and-security/warnings.md) add/remove interface.

***

## Toggle Welcome Bypass

If a user is marked as exempt from the [Welcome Role](../settings-menu/user-engagement/welcome-role.md), removes the bypass and vice versa.
