# 📧 Messages

## Clone Message

Makes an exact copy of a message in the specified channel. The new author of the message will be Starbot. Surveys and role menus will maintain functionality.

***

## Delete

Deletes a message. Users can delete their own survey responses with this.

***

## Edit

Pulls up the edit menu, which differs depending on the message that was selected. Types of editable messages include:\
・<mark style="color:$primary;">Surveys</mark>\
・<mark style="color:$primary;">Survey responses</mark> (only the user who submitted it)\
・<mark style="color:$primary;">Lotteries</mark>\
・<mark style="color:$primary;">Role menus</mark>\
・<mark style="color:$primary;">Mod logs</mark> (only the mod who made it, and only up to 3 days after)

***

## Embedify

Converts a plain message into an embed. The new author will be Starbot, but the original author's name and icon will preserved at the top of the embed. Allows you to preview before creating.
